Cube World

Party sports on a tiny planet. Your phone is the controller.

How to Play

  1. Choose a game first Press Play, then spin the cube with the mouse and click a side (or use ← →). Each side is a different sport.
  2. Then join with your phones Once a game is chosen, a QR code appears. Scan it with the phone camera, or open the address shown and type the room code. Phones must be on the same Wi-Fi as this computer.
  3. Make your player Type your name, pick your colours and tap Join. Your character appears on the cube.
  4. Play! Press Play or Enter. Swing your phone like a putter in Minigolf, like a racket in Badminton.

Phone can't connect? The first time, the phone shows a security warning: tap "Show Details" or "Advanced" and continue. If the Mac asks whether "node" may accept incoming connections, click "Allow".
