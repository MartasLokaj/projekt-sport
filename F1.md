# Formula racing (SPEC §16) — brief for Claude Code

Read this whole file, SPEC.md (§12 hub, §15, §16), SKI.md and progress/STATUS.md before starting. Talk to the user in
Czech, in plain words; all code and in-game text in English. Plan first, wait for the user's OK, build phase by phase,
test on real phones after each phase, commit, push and update progress/STATUS.md and progress/OVERVIEW.md.

## Why
The user (2026-11-11) loves the speed of the skiing race and wants a **single-seater circuit race in the spirit of
Formula 1**: a few laps on a twisty track, overtaking, a pit stop, a chequered flag. **Same steering as skiing: the phone
held sideways like a steering wheel.** Inspiration only: no real team, driver, sponsor, circuit or series names or
logos ("Formula 1", "F1", team liveries…) anywhere in the game. In-game name e.g. **"Grand Prix"** or **"Open-wheel
Racing"**; cars are our own toy design in the player's colour.

## Look
Same toy-like, soft 3D family as minigolf, beach and ski. Bright summer afternoon.
1. **Toy cars.** Chunky, rounded open-wheel cars built in code: a fat cigar body, big front and rear wings, four oversized
   wheels (cylinders), a halo bar, the driver = our chibi with a helmet in the player's colour and a number on the nose.
   Slightly "squashed" proportions (big wheels, short body) so they read well from far away.
2. **Circuit.** Grey asphalt ribbon generated from a closed spline (like minigolf lanes and the ski run), red-white kerbs
   on the corners, white lines, green grass, gravel traps (beige), tyre walls (stacked tori), grandstands made of boxes
   with tiny confetti-coloured crowds (instanced), a start/finish gantry with 5 red lights, flags, a pit lane with garages.
3. **Atmosphere.** Warm low sun, long shadows, heat haze above the asphalt (cheap shimmer only if fps holds), balloons
   and a blimp in the sky, fireworks at the finish.
4. **Speed.** Low chase camera, slight FOV widening at top speed, speed lines, sparks when the car bottoms out on the
   straight, tyre smoke when braking hard, skid marks (reuse the ski `tracks.ts` idea), rubber pieces and dust off the
   track.

**No modelling needed.** Everything from primitives, instanced where repeated. Free CC0 packs only if code shapes are not
enough, and **always state file name, source and exact size and get the user's yes before downloading anything.**

## Controls (phone)
- **Steering = the ski wheel steering** (`controller/ski/steer.ts`, `WheelSteer`): reuse it, do not copy it. Calibration
  (Ready), Re-centre button, dead zone, smoothing, response curve, per-player sensitivity, both landscape orientations.
  Tuning in `TUNING.f1.steer` (cars need a bit more lock at low speed and less at high speed: speed-sensitive steering).
- **Thumbs:** right thumb = big **GAS** pad, left thumb = big **BRAKE** pad. Default **auto-gas** for beginners (the car
  accelerates by itself, BRAKE slows it), switch in the lobby card ("Assist: On/Off").
- **DRS / Boost** button: a short speed boost, only usable in marked zones on the straights when you are close behind
  another car (shows a green light on the phone). Creates fun overtakes.
- **Pit stop minigame:** when a player drives into the pit lane, the phone shows 4 tyres; **shake the phone** (or tap the
  tyres fast) to change them. Faster = shorter stop. Teammates/other phones may help (party moment).
- Vibration: kerbs (light buzz), collisions, the 5 lights going out, finish.
- Send ~30× per second, latest value wins (same as `ski_steer`). Mac keys: ← → steer, ↑ gas, ↓ brake, Shift boost,
  P pit, R restart.

## Race
- **Quick 3 laps / Normal 5 / Long 8**, a lap ~30–40 s. Standing start: 5 red lights go on one by one, then out = GO.
  Jumping the start = 2 s penalty.
- **Arcade physics**, not a simulator: grip that drops slightly when you steer hard at high speed (light drift), slower
  on grass and gravel, walls push you back and slow you (no damage, no retiring). Slipstream: driving right behind
  another car gives a little extra speed.
- **Tyres:** grip slowly drops over laps (small, visible on the phone as a tyre colour green→yellow→red). One pit stop
  restores it. Makes the pit stop a real choice without being complicated. Can be switched off for Quick races.
- 1 phone = vs CPU cars, 2–4 phones = everyone races, **CPU fills the grid to 8 cars** (more cars = more overtaking,
  instanced so it stays cheap). CPU level Easy/Normal/Pro from the card, reuse `host/ski/cpu.ts` ideas (racing line
  following + mistakes on Easy).
- **One shared camera** following the leading human player, like skiing; humans who fall too far behind get a gentle
  catch-up (slipstream stronger behind) and respawn on track after a big crash. Optional later: TV-style **broadcast
  camera** switching between battles (side view, helicopter view) for replays.
- **Live standings tower** on the left (positions, gaps), lap counter, "Fastest lap" purple flash.
- Finish: chequered flag, the camera swings round, champagne spray on our existing podium.
- Later ideas (not now): rain race (wet tyres, spray), safety car, several circuits (city streets with tight walls,
  seaside, night race with lights), qualifying lap, championship over 3 races, replays.

## Track
- Generated from a **closed spline with control points** (handcrafted lists in `host/f1/tracks.ts`, like `HOLES` in
  minigolf): 1 circuit at first, ~1 km, with a long straight, a hairpin, an S-chicane, a fast sweeping corner, a bridge or
  tunnel for fun. Width ~3 cars.
- Racing line stored per track (for CPU and an optional faint "braking zone" marker for beginners).
- Checkpoints along the spline to count laps and stop shortcuts (skipping a corner = respawn + 1 s).

## Performance
Target **60 fps on the user's MacBook Air (Apple Silicon)** with 8 cars. Instanced crowd, trees, kerbs and tyre walls,
track built once (it is a loop, no chunks needed), one shadow-casting sun with a tight shadow camera on the camera
target, particle counts in `TUNING.f1`. Measure and report frame times after each phase.

## Architecture
- New game in the registry (`host/hub/faces.ts`): it takes a mystery side of the cube. Code in `host/f1/`
  (`Race.ts` rules/camera/standings, `track.ts` + `tracks.ts` circuit, `car.ts` `CarPhysics` (testable in Node, like
  `RacerPhysics`) and the toy car model, `cpu.ts`, `pit.ts`), tuning `TUNING.f1` in `shared/tuning.ts`, phone screen in
  `controller/f1/` (reuses `controller/ski/steer.ts`), new messages in `shared/protocol.ts` (`f1_mode`,
  `f1_input {steer, gas, brake, boost}`, `f1_pit`, `f1_cue`), server mode flag like `ski_mode`.
- Reuse lobby, seats, characters (helmet outfit), sensitivity, podium, sounds, transitions, ski steering and ski tracks.
- Do not change Minigolf, Badminton, Curling or Skiing behaviour. If `steer.ts` needs a change for cars, keep skiing
  exactly the same (new options with the old default).
- Log input samples to `logs/f1-input.jsonl` (gitignored).
